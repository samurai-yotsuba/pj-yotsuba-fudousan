**Key points:** A company can use freee人事労務 to process its own payroll and filings, or engage a Shakai Hoken Roumushi, a Japanese Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant, to perform the work. Software functions and the professional qualifications to accept another company's filing work are different matters. 四葉社会保険労務士事務所 offers the **HR Outsourcing Plan, covering freee人事労務, LINE time clock, payroll, routine filings and labor advice**. For 1–3 people on the payroll, fees are **from 33,000 yen per month, including tax / initial setup and workflow design from 88,000 yen, including tax**.

After adopting freee, the difficult question is often who checks the facts and how much work to entrust to someone else, rather than which button to press. Distinguishing software use, the consultant's scope and company approval makes monthly work easier to organize.

## Can freee HR also handle electronic filing?

freee's official help describes an electronic filing function for corporate establishments. Conditions such as gBizID apply; check the official help for supported documents and eligible plans. The same help page states that **filing by a Shakai Hoken Roumushi as a representative is not supported** (updated April 10, 2026; checked September 9, 2026).

Distinguish the function through which a company submits its own filings from the arrangements through which a consultant submits documents on a client's behalf. When engaging a consultant to handle filings, separately establish the submission method and the necessary authorization and checking steps. Using freee does not make every submission route the same.

Features change. Check [freee's official help](https://support.freee.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/7277508582297) to confirm whether your company can submit the particular filing itself.

## Does having the software let you handle another company's filings?

Article 2, paragraph 1 of Japan's Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Act defines work including preparation of labor and social insurance application documents, submission services, administrative representation, preparation of statutory books and documents, and labor-related consultation and guidance. Article 27 generally restricts persons other than Shakai Hoken Roumushi and other authorized persons from carrying out, as a business for remuneration at another person's request, the work in Article 2, paragraph 1, items 1 through 2. Exceptions under other laws and provisions may apply; a qualified professional checks whether an individual engagement can be accepted.

This restriction is distinct from a company processing its own payroll and filings. It is also inaccurate to describe all payroll calculation or all general consultation and guidance as work reserved exclusively for a Shakai Hoken Roumushi. However, the points requiring confirmation change when the outsourced work includes preparation of statutory documents or submission services.

Do not choose a provider only on whether they can use freee. Confirm which documents they will prepare, where they will submit them, and what they will do in whose name.

## How is monthly work divided when 四葉 handles it?

The HR Outsourcing Plan includes freee人事労務 Standard, LINE time clock, online payslips, monthly payroll processing, updates such as social insurance rates, and routine labor advice. Routine filings cover enrollment and withdrawal, dependent changes, monthly remuneration change reports, bonus payment reports, annual remuneration reports and annual labor insurance renewal.

| Work entrusted to 四葉 | Work the company performs | Separately priced work |
|---|---|---|
| Payroll and routine filings | Checking, correcting and finalizing attendance | Initial social insurance registration, establishment of labor insurance coverage and employment insurance establishment registration |
| Everyday labor advice; advice and review on hiring terms | Providing personnel change information and obtaining necessary internal approvals | Residence status applications, year-end tax adjustment and full recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) |
| Basic advice on employing foreign nationals and basic explanations in Chinese | Final approval of payroll results | Specialist translation and extended interpreting |

**The company is responsible for checking and finalizing attendance and giving final approval of payroll results.** For example, when a time entry is missing, the company checks the actual work performed, corrects and finalizes attendance, and payroll is then processed using that data. AI or software does not infer the actual hours worked and finalize them.

## What does the initial fee cover?

Fees are **from 33,000 yen per month for 1–3 people on the payroll, including tax / initial setup and workflow design from 88,000 yen, including tax**. The initial fee includes freee configuration, LINE time clock setup, pay structure and allowance/deduction settings, employee registration, social insurance information, the first payroll test and workflow design.

The first run establishes the cutoff date, the deadline for reporting changes, the person responsible for checking and the final approval process, as well as the calculation results. Initial implementation is not offered as a free configuration service included in the monthly fee.

Monthly fees are 44,000 yen for 4–5 people, 55,000 yen for 6–10, and 55,000 yen plus 3,300 yen for each person above 10 for 11 or more people. All prices include tax. Headcount bands reflect the volume of payroll, filings, usage capacity and inquiries. The [fee schedule](/en/labor/ryokin) shows the monthly and initial fees together.

## Are year-end tax adjustment and residence status applications included in the same contract?

A feature being available in freee does not mean our office accepts the corresponding engagement. freee会計 is not included in the HR Outsourcing Plan. For tax representation, preparation of tax documents and tax advice, we guide clients to engage a Japanese tax accountant directly. This does not mean that every bookkeeping task is reserved exclusively for tax accountants. ## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Can we consult you if we already use freee?**
A. Yes. We check your current plan, settings and contracts, then identify what can continue and what needs changing. The contracting party and data transfer conditions are confirmed before signing.

**Q. Does the consultant file as our representative from within freee?**
A. The official help checked on September 9, 2026 states that representative filing by a Shakai Hoken Roumushi is not supported. The submission method for an outsourced filing is confirmed separately.

**Q. Does the monthly fee include both payroll and routine filings?**
A. The HR Outsourcing Plan includes both. Initial insurance registration, residence status applications and similar work are charged separately. The company finalizes attendance and gives final approval of payroll results.

**Q. Is the initial fee included in the monthly fee?**
A. A separate initial setup and workflow design fee starts at 88,000 yen, including tax. It covers freee configuration through the first payroll test and workflow design, and is presented at the same time as the monthly fee.
